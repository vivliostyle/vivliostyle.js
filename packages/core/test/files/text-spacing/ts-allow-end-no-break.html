The polyfill sets a space after closing punctuation to adjust it at the end of a line, and the browser breaks the line after the space before any character. The lines marked “no break” must keep 「こ。」 with the character after it, as the browser does without the polyfill: the line may not break before U+2060 WORD JOINER, U+FEFF, U+00A0, 「……」 or 「！」. The lines marked “break” or “hangs” break after 「。」.
